# Ecofire Lara component references

[README](../README.md) · [boards](boards.md) · [references](references.md)

This is a consolidated identification guide, not a reproduced catalogue.
References below come from public supplier listings and technical sheets recorded
on 2026-10-10. Supplier compatibility claims are not proof of what is installed:
check the stove serial number, motor label, dimensions and connector before ordering.
Original PDFs, exploded views and website copies remain in the private reference repo.

## Fans and their distinct roles

| Component | Candidate reference | Public evidence and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Room-air convection fan | Palazzetti `895706540` | [GED listing](https://www.granules-eco-developpement.fr/produit/palazzetti-ventilateur-895706540/) names Lara and describes a radial fan; installed label unconfirmed |
| Room-air convection fan | Palazzetti `895700630`, EBM `D2E097-BE01-02` | [Larel listing](https://larel.com/b2b/en/ebm/96-14706013.html) associates these codes and Lara; equivalence to `895706540` is not established |
| Room-air fan support | Palazzetti `895704420` | [Supplier listing](https://www.piecesxpress.com/article-388499-510458-bride-de-ventil-de-chauffage-ambiant-d2e-895704420-palazzetti.htm); this is the support, not the fan |
| Combustion-gas extractor | Palazzetti `895700600`, EBM `R2E150-AN91-13` | [Xodostore listing](https://xodostore.com/prodotto/estrattore-fumi-originale-cod-895700600/) names Lara and the EBM suffix |
| Extractor replacement candidate | Palazzetti `895755190` | [Supplier listing](https://xodostore.com/en/prodotto/original-fume-extractor-for-pellet-stoves-palazzetti-and-royal-code-895755190/); kit contents and interchangeability need checking |

The convection fan distributes room heat; the extractor moves flue gas.
Tangential and centrifugal fan geometries are not interchangeable descriptions.
The auger gearmotor is a separate component, not a third fan.

The public [AN91-13 technical sheet](https://www.fansco.com/datasheets/ebmpapst/R2E150-AN91-13.pdf)
lists 230 V, 50 Hz, 32 W, 2400 rpm and a **1 uF / 400 VDB** capacitor.
The related [AN91-01 sheet](https://ebmpapst-ventilator.hu/userfiles/webshop/products/attachments/R2E150-AN91-01.pdf)
does not establish suffix interchangeability. A previously suggested 4 uF capacitor
is not supported by either sheet. No mains bench-test or inferred wiring procedure
is endorsed here; service work requires a qualified technician and exact model data.

## Seals and handle hardware

The public [Lara parts listing](https://www.piecesxpress.com/nomenclature-9338-0-pieces-detachees-poele-palazzetti-lara.htm)
distinguishes 6 mm cord `895701030`, 8 mm cord `895701040`, complete handle
`895700550`, spring-equipped eccentric pin `895700430` and locking pin `892600100`.
These references describe different positions/components, not interchangeable alternatives.

[Gnesato's locking-pin listing](https://www.gnesato.com/fr/pi%C3%A8ces-de-rechange/cat%C3%A9gories/vis-et-petites-pi%C3%A8ces/goupille-de-fermeture-de-poign%C3%A9e-m6x22mm-892600100/)
specifies M6 x 22 mm, but those dimensions alone do not identify the shoulder,
head or latch geometry. No standard screw substitute is verified.
An extractor insulating disc is not automatically a housing gasket; bearing
and gasket references for the installed motor remain unconfirmed.

## Identification record

Record the complete part label, stove product/serial number, mounting dimensions,
rotation/viewing convention, electrical rating and connector arrangement.
Keep uncertain replacements marked as candidates. Do not infer compatibility
from a shared family number, a seller's generic photo or another stove's repair.
The [references index](references.md) lists broader model and technical resources.
